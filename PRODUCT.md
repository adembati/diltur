# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript – no framework dependencies. Responsive design optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile viewports.

## Users

**Primary:** Travel agencies and B2B buyers seeking to book tour packages or inquire about group rates and educational programs.

**Secondary:** Individual tourists planning personal trips to Turkey, discovering destinations and tour options.

**Tertiary:** Educational institutions and language learners researching combined language education + travel packages.

All visitors arrive with varying familiarity with Turkish destinations and varying commitment levels (browsing vs. actively booking).

## Product Purpose

Türkiye Turları connects tour operators with travel agencies and individual travelers by offering:
- A curated portfolio of Turkish destinations (Kapadokya, Trabzon, İstanbul, Bursa, Antalya, Bodrum, Pamukkale, Efes)
- Integrated language education programs alongside tours
- Professional, trustworthy information architecture to support booking decisions and inquiries

Success means agencies find what they need to book, individuals discover inspiring trips, and language learners see education + travel as an integrated offering.

## Positioning

**Unique mechanism:** Kombiniert language education with destination tours as a bundled offering, rather than treating them as separate products. This positions Türkiye Turları as a lifestyle and learning platform, not just a booking agent.

**Claim:** "Türkiye'yi keşfetmenin en güzel yolu" (The most beautiful way to discover Turkey) – emphasizing curated quality and cultural immersion, differentiated from generic tour aggregators.

## Operating Context

- **Primary workflow:** User arrives → browses tours or language programs → filters/compares → contacts via form or calls
- **Secondary workflow:** Agencies inquire about group rates, special packages, or language programs; filled via contact form
- **Content needs:** Real tour itineraries, pricing, images/descriptions, team member bios, FAQ, blog for SEO
- **Business operations:** 7/24 support contact channels; content ownership by tour coordinator and marketing manager

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Current capabilities:**
- 8 tour packages with descriptions, pricing, duration, highlights
- Language education feature section
- Blog with travel tips and destination guides
- Contact form with routing (tours vs. inquiries)
- Responsive mobile/tablet/desktop
- Social media footer links
- Newsletter subscription

**Technical constraints:**
- Static HTML/CSS/JS – no backend database, no real payment processing, no user authentication
- Contact forms submit client-side only (no email integration yet)
- Blog and tour data are hardcoded (future: CMS integration)

**Terminology:**
- "Turlar" (Tours) = packaged destination trips
- "Dil Eğitimi" (Language Education) = integrated language learning + immersion programs
- "Rehber" (Guide) = professional tour guide
- "Rezervasyon" (Reservation) = booking/inquiry

**Undecided facts:**
- Backend booking/payment system (future phase)
- Custom photography/videography of actual tours, guides, and destinations (current destination photos are licensed stock, not original)
- Video content
- Multi-language support (currently Turkish only)
- Advanced filtering or personalization

## Brand Commitments

**Name:** Türkiye Turları (Turkey Tours)

**Voice:** Professional, warm, inviting. Position as trusted local experts who understand Turkey deeply. Tone is aspirational but grounded ("discover," "experience," "immerse") rather than sales-pushy.

**Visual identity:** None pre-established. Design should feel premium and trustworthy (suited to B2B travel agencies and affluent individual travelers), with Turkish cultural touchstones (Turkish flag red #C41E3A as primary, warm golds and blues) without becoming costume-like.

**Personality:** Knowledgeable, enthusiastic, accessible. The brand is about revealing Turkey's beauty and diversity, not about corporate formality.

## Product Principles

1. **Trustworthy expertise:** Every surface should signal local knowledge and professional care. Real guides, real itineraries, real customer confidence.
2. **Integrated lifestyle:** Language education and tours belong together. The site architecture reflects this positioning, not as an afterthought add-on.
3. **Clear decision-making:** Visitors should know what they're getting (duration, price, highlights, activities) at a glance. Comparison and choice should be effortless.
4. **Responsive accessibility:** Works flawlessly on agency office desktops, tourist mobile phones, and tablet browsing. No friction across devices.
5. **Warm, earned confidence:** Visual and verbal tone should feel like talking to a knowledgeable friend, not a corporate booking engine.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No accessibility standard has been explicitly mandated yet. Site should follow web best practices (WCAG 2.1 AA) for color contrast, keyboard navigation, semantic HTML, alt text, and form labeling to remain inclusive to diverse users (including those with visual or motor impairments, non-native Turkish speakers where applicable).
